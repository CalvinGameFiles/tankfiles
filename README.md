# TankFiles

A single-player, Wii Play Tanks style game by CalvinGameFiles. HTML5 canvas, played on the PC for testing and wrapped as an Android app later (same `www/` folder, like FNAF Siege).

## Play on the PC
Double-click **TankFiles** on the desktop (or `Play TankFiles.bat`). It starts a tiny Node server and opens the game in its own window. Close the console window to stop it.

## Layout
- `www/` the game. `index.html` holds the menus, `js/game.js` the arena/tank/shells/mines, `js/ui.js` the menus and save flow, `js/store.js` the 3 save slots + options, `js/sfx.js` placeholder sounds plus `Music`, which plays `www/audio/music/<map style>.mp3` (indoor, grass, snow, sand, beach, volcano, factory, battle, grave): looping through a mission, held while paused, stopped on death or a cleared mission.
- `scripts/make-icon.py` draws the app icon and the title tank. `scripts/make-shortcut.ps1` puts the desktop shortcut back.
- `debug/smoke.py` drives the real page in headless Chrome and checks the whole menu/save/arena flow. Screenshots land in `debug/shots/`.

## Controls
PC: WASD / arrows drive, mouse aims, click fires, space or right-click lays a mine, Esc pauses, R retries after a death.
Touch: left thumb drives, right thumb aims and fires on release, MINE button, pause button top-right.

## Rules so far
Shells bounce off walls once, cancel each other, and your own ricochet can get you. Five shells and two mines out at a time. Mines arm after a second, go off near another tank (never the tank that laid them), on a shell, or after ten seconds, and break wooden crates. A destroyed tank leaves smoke, then an X on the floor (blue for the player).

## Levels
`www/js/levels.js` holds every mission. A mission is either a wall list (`[type, col, row, across, tall]`, so "1x4" = 1 across, 4 tall) or a drawn map: rows of characters, one per square, `#` stone, `W` wood, `w` hole (shells fly over, tanks cannot drive in), `S` silver platform, `/` ramp, `.` floor, `P` player, `D` dark brown, `B` light brown, `C` cyan, `Y` yellow, `R` red, `G` lime green, `U` purple, `H` white (invisible), `K` black, `T` silver auto turret, `O` oil bomber, `~` water, `L` lava; a lowercase tank letter (or `p`) stands that tank up on a platform; a tank letter with water on three or four sides is standing in the sea. `floor: 'grass'` makes a mission outdoors, `floor: 'snow'` a rocky mountainside with snow-covered squares, `floor: 'sand'` a desert with sandstone walls and cacti in place of crates, `floor: 'beach'` pale sand with sea water squares: tanks drive 30% slower through water, a mine that goes off in water breaks wood but hurts no tank, and a white tank wading through water loses its invisibility until it climbs out. `floor: 'volcano'` is red-brown rock with black stone walls, dirt heaps for crates and lava: a tank whose centre reaches the middle of a lava square burns (grazing an edge is fine); black tanks are lava-proof; the others never path through lava, except that a tank dodging a shell with nowhere else to go will back onto a lava square's edge rather than be hit. `floor: 'factory'` is grey steel plate with steel walls, and every `W` is a barrel coloured at random when the mission loads: 60% blue (just breaks), 20% green (floods the 3x3 round it with acid, which burns like lava), 20% black (floods the 3x3 with tar, half speed for every tank). An oil bomber that dies leaves a 5x5 yellow oil slick that slows only the player, by 40%.

Silver platforms are a level up: a tank gets on or off only over a ramp square, a shell fired from the ground stops at a platform's side (it can go up a ramp), a shell fired from on top drops to the ground when it leaves the slab, and a mine's blast hits tanks on either level. `python -I scripts/check-maze.py` checks every drawn map (row widths, border, every tank can reach the player).

Enemy tanks: light brown (stationary, slow shots), dark brown (moves, bank shots), cyan (fast rockets, no bounce), yellow (quick, lays mines and runs clear), red (rapid fire, three shells out at once, almost as fast as you and it closes in), lime green (stationary, two fast rockets out at once that bounce twice, very accurate), purple (five shells out, nearly your speed, charges in and lays mines), white (a slower purple that cannot be seen except by its tracks, smoke and shells, or while it wades through water); white tanks lay no mines; black (your speed, two fast no-bounce rockets, no mines, drives through lava and acid), silver auto turret (a red tank that cannot move), oil bomber (pitch black, no turret, no gun, the black tank's speed; it charges you and goes off like a mine on contact, and leaves oil when it dies). Every tank simulates a shot before firing and never takes one that bounces back into itself or a friend; a yellow only drops a mine when it has a route out of the blast.

Title menu: PLAY (three save files), LEVEL SELECT (one button per mission in `levels.js`, auto-updated, coloured by map style: blue indoors, green grass, white snow, yellow desert, aqua beach, red volcano, grey factory; `UI.LEVEL_SELECT_LOCKED` in `ui.js` locks it until every mission has been cleared once, off for now), OPTIONS.

Tests: `python -I debug/smoke.py` (headless Chrome, screenshots in `debug/shots/`), `python -I debug/diag-yellow.py` (watches the mine layers on mission 10), `python -I debug/diag-ramp.py` (drives onto a ramp from its edges and checks the tank is never wedged).

Battlefield (Missions 81-90, `floor: 'battle'`): dead yellow-brown grass, olive bunker walls, plain wood crates. Each load scatters 1-6 grey landmines (`seedMines`): no timer, they go off when any tank comes near or anything shoots them. Orange tanks (grid letter `F`) are flame throwers: the yellow tank's speed, no mines, one or two flame WAVES every 3 s. A wave is five fire cells abreast, about two squares wide; each cell dies on the first wall, crosses holes, kills any tank, sets off mines, and loses one cell to every shell that hits it (five hits and the wave is gone). Mission 88 is the larger 30 x 20 field and Mission 90 the extra large 36 x 24 one (the view scales to fit). Level select colour: orange.

Graveyard (Missions 91-100, `floor: 'grave'`): purple grass, dark crypt stone, tombstones as the breakable block, and a night wash (`drawNight`) that is lighter round the player; shells, fire and explosions are drawn over it so they glow. Dark pine green tanks (DPG, grid letter `N`, type `pine`) are lime greens that drive at half speed: two fast rockets out, two bounces each. Missions 94, 97, 98 and 100 are extra large (36 x 24). Level select colour: purple.

Multiplayer (title -> MULTIPLAYER: LOCAL over Wi-Fi for 2-10, or SPLIT SCREEN for 2; `www/js/versus.js` menus, `www/js/net.js` link, `Game.versus` in game.js). PREPARE YOUR TANK: one of 10 models (`TANK_MODELS` in custom.js, drawn by `drawModel` in game.js: standard, buggy, goliath, spiker, twin gun, hover, stinger, gatling, dozer, beetle; same hitbox for all, every model paints with the same 9 parts), the colour of the X it leaves, TWO WEAPONS of your choice (shell, rocket, bouncy rocket, heat seeker, frost, flame thrower, super laser; each counts its own shots in the air, only 2 bouncy rockets at once) and up to 2 powers (mines, lava/mine immunity, teleport, invisibility, speed). Weapon 1: left click / F, or let go of the aim stick; weapon 2: right click / Q, or the second fire button. Split screen: blue F / R, red / and right shift. GAME RULES (`cleanRules` in custom.js): BATTLE (1-10 lives, last one standing), SLAYER (no lives, first to 10-100 kills in steps of 5, 5-30 min or no limit; most kills if time runs out), SLAUGHTER (1-30 min, most kills wins; a tie is a draw). TEAMS (Local only): 2 teams (blue/red), 4 with exactly 8 players (+ green/yellow), 5 with 10 (+ purple); players pick their team in the lobby, a team is full at its share and START stays locked until the teams are even; no friendly fire, team kills add up. In battle: the arrow tab on the right edge (or Tab) slides out the LEADERBOARD over half the screen: players by kills, gold / silver / bronze for the top three. Test: `python -I debug/versus2.py`; `debug/models-sheet.py` draws all 10 models big.

Level creator (title -> LEVEL CREATOR, `www/js/editor.js`): an empty map whose size changes with the +/- buttons (8-40 across, 6-28 tall). BLOCKS tab: walls, breakable blocks, holes, platforms, ramps, water, lava, acid, tar, oil and purple TELEPORT squares (1-10); drag to paint, right-click or the eraser clears. TANKS tab: the player, every campaign tank, and custom tanks (+ CREATE TANK, the pencil edits one). STYLE tab: any map style plus a colour for the floor, walls, breakable blocks, holes, platforms and each liquid (tinted in `drawWallsTinted`). SAVE / LOAD / NEW keep levels in localStorage; TEST plays the level (`Game.playCustom`) and returns to the editor. Tank creator: paint each part (treads, pads, hull edge, hull, lamp, barrel, muzzle, turret ring, turret top) from a colour grid or any colour, speed 1-120%, HP 1-10 (hearts over the tank) and powers from `www/js/custom.js` (`addPower` keeps the rules: one weapon, one multi shot, Suicide only with Suicide Dropper, Load Tank only with its options). Custom tanks become `TANK_TYPES['c_<id>']`. Engine powers: rocket, bouncy rocket, multi shot 1-5, flame thrower, heat seeking rocket (`homingAim`), frost shot (`freezeAt`, 20 s), super laser (`laserBrain` / `updateBeams`), mines, suicide, suicide dropper (`spillBox`), stand still, invisibility (any liquid reveals), enchanter (`updateEnchanters`), war train + load tanks (`linkTrains` / `updateTrains` / `cutTrain`), resistance, teleportation (pads are linked in `bfs`, `escapeByPad`) and lava, mine and friendly-fire immunity; every hit goes through `hitTank`. Tests: `debug/creator.py`, screenshots `debug/editor-look.py`.
